For hotels etc the following procedures apply:

Once you're back you need to write to Kwestura (rozliczenia@adm.uw.edu.pl) in order to make the travel settlement. You need to provide the following information/attachments

1) date and hour of the start and the end of your journey (sometimes, when it was "a ground" journey/ex. by train/ they ask about a moment of crossing the Polish boarder) 
2) the invoice for the hotel, conference fee (if paid by you), other costs (if any) 
The invoice data

Invoice should contain your name and the dates of the hotel stay 

3) the bank confirmation for the hotel and other invoiced expenses
4) the statement about the meals provided (number of breakfasts/lunches/dinners)
5) if you did use local public transport (it is a flat rate: 10% of daily allowance x numer of days, if you used local public transport; tickets paid via travelbank don't count)
